The difference

Written for the BCEA, not translated into it.

International leave products treat South Africa as a settings file. These are the places where that assumption breaks, and where a system built here doesn't.

BCEA s22

Sick leave runs on 36 months

Not a calendar year. Six weeks of sick leave across a three-year cycle, and where that cycle starts differs per employee. Systems that reset in January get this wrong for everybody.

Public Holidays Act 36 of 1994

Twelve holidays, and the Sunday rule

Freedom Day, Youth Day, Heritage Day, Day of Reconciliation — plus the rule that a holiday falling on a Sunday shifts to the Monday. Loaded and excluded from leave counts automatically.

BCEA s27

Family responsibility is its own thing

Three days a cycle, for employees with four months' service working four or more days a week. It is not annual leave and not compassionate leave, and it should not come off the wrong balance.

BCEA s23

The certificate rule, enforced

More than two consecutive days, or a third absence inside eight weeks, and a certificate is required. The system asks at the point of applying rather than three weeks later at payroll.

BCEA s20

Annual leave in working days

Twenty-one consecutive days is fifteen working days on a normal five-day week. Getting that translation wrong by a few days across thirty staff is a real cost.

POPIA

Employee data stays accounted for

Sick notes and ID numbers are personal information. Access is by role, changes are logged, and data can be exported or removed on request — which matters the day someone asks.
