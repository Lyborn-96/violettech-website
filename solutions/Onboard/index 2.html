What it replaces

The first day tells them what kind of company this is.

Onboarding is the one process where the cost of getting it wrong is visible to the person it happened to — and they remember it for years.

How it works now

First Monday
  • A WhatsApp group is created the Friday before, and half the tasks live only in one person's head
  • The laptop is ordered on day two because nobody told IT
  • The contract is signed in week three, after work has already started
  • Identity documents sit in an email inbox and a filing cabinet, indefinitely
  • UIF and tax details reach payroll after the first pay run, so the first payslip is wrong
  • Nobody realises the visa expires in four months until it has expired
  • The next new starter goes through exactly the same thing, because nothing was written down

How it works after

Same Monday
  • The checklist is generated the moment the offer is accepted, with owners and due dates
  • IT, finance and the manager each see only their own tasks
  • The contract is issued and signed before day one, because the system will not report ready without it
  • Documents are stored once, access-controlled, and logged when viewed
  • Payroll has what it needs before the cut-off, so the first payslip is right
  • Visa and permit expiry dates are tracked from day one
  • The next starter runs off the same template, improved
The difference

Onboarding software that knows which country it is in.

International onboarding tools give you a task list and a welcome video. They do not know what the BCEA requires on day one, and they have never heard of a UI-19.

BCEA s29

Written particulars, on day one

Not "within the first month" and not "when we get to it". The employee must receive written particulars of employment when they commence work. The system treats an unsigned contract as a blocker, not a task.

Immigration Act

Right to work is verified, not assumed

Employing a foreign national without a valid work authorisation carries consequences for the employer. Tick the box and the verification task appears, with the permit expiry tracked from that day forward.

UI Act · Income Tax Act

Payroll gets what it needs before cut-off

UIF declaration and employees' tax details have deadlines that fall in the first month. Miss the cut-off and the first payslip is wrong — which is the single most common thing new employees complain about.

Employment Equity Act

Declaration, not interrogation

Equity self-declaration is voluntary and cannot be compelled. The system records it as offered and optionally declined, which is the distinction that matters if reporting is ever questioned.

COIDA · OHS Act

Covered and inducted before work starts

Injury-on-duty cover and a safety induction relevant to the actual role. In operational businesses this is the item most often skipped and most expensive to have skipped.

POPIA

The most sensitive file you will ever hold

Identity number, bank account, medical scheme, equity declaration — all in one place. Role-based access, logged views, and a retention rule, because a shared drive is not a lawful basis.
