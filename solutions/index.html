Solutions

Products built for South African labour law.

International HR platforms treat South African compliance as a configuration option. We are building systems where the BCEA, public holiday rules and POPIA obligations are structural, not bolted on afterwards.

Currently in development

Three systems, open for pilot deployment.

These are products we are actively building, not client work. If one fits a problem you have, we are taking a small number of pilot clients at reduced rates in exchange for feedback and a reference.

Leave Management System

DEMO AVAILABLE

A leave engine that gets South African rules right. Annual, sick, family responsibility, maternity and parental leave with correct BCEA accrual cycles. Working-day calculations that account for public holidays including the Sunday rule. Approval chains with delegation for when a manager is away, and an audit log behind every decision.

  • BCEA-correct accrual and cycle handling
  • Public holiday and working-day engine
  • Multi-level approval with delegation
  • Employee self-service and balance visibility
  • Full audit trail on every transaction
