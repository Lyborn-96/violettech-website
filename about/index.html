About the company

Smart solutions. Stronger tomorrows.

VioletTech Solutions is a registered South African technology company based in Midrand, Gauteng. We build business software for organisations that need it to work on the first day it goes live — and to keep working long after we have handed it over.

Who we are

A software company built around delivery, not sales.

VioletTech Solutions was founded on a pattern seen from the inside of large organisations: critical processes running on paper forms and spreadsheets, replacement systems quoted at prices that bore no relation to the work involved, and software left behind that nobody could maintain once the original developer moved on.

We built the company to answer all three. Our engagements are scoped by the people who will build them, priced against a written design rather than a guess, and handed over with documentation that lets your own team take it from there.

We are deliberately selective about how much work we take at once. That is a constraint we have chosen, and it is the reason nothing is lost in translation between the first conversation and the deployment.

Who we build for

Public sector departments Mining & resources Manufacturing Recruitment & staffing Logistics Financial services Growing SMEs

What we stand on

Three commitments we put in writing.

These are not values on a wall. They are terms we agree to at the start of every engagement.

A design before a price

We will not quote a build until the data model, the security roles and the licensing are agreed in writing. If the honest answer is that the system should not be built, we say so at that point — before you commit budget.

You own what we build

Source code, documentation, environments and admin access are yours. Your system should never depend on one supplier being reachable, and we build on that assumption.

Built for South African rules

BCEA leave calculations, public holiday handling, POPIA obligations and audit trails are designed in from the start — not configured in afterwards to satisfy an auditor.

Who you deal with

No account managers in the middle.

You will not be introduced to one person and handed to another. The people who sit in your process-mapping session are the people who write the code, run the deployment and train your administrators.

It is the reason we keep the number of concurrent projects low, and the reason nothing gets lost between the first conversation and go-live. When you raise an issue six months later, you are talking to someone who remembers why the system was built that way.
