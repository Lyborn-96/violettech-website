About

Smart solutions. Stronger tomorrows.

VioletTech Solutions is a registered South African technology company based in Midrand, Gauteng, built on a simple observation: the organisations that need good internal systems the most are usually the ones being quoted the least realistic prices for them.

The company

Small on purpose.

Larger firms solve the capacity problem by putting a junior developer on your project and a senior name on the invoice. We solve it by taking fewer projects. The person who scopes your system is the person who builds it and the person who hands it over.

That limits how much work we can take at once, which is deliberate. It also means nothing is lost in translation between the sales conversation and the deployment, and there is never a question about who understands the code.

We work with public sector departments, growing businesses, and teams who have outgrown the spreadsheet that used to be fine.

Lyborn Kurhula Nemo

Founder & Solution Architect

VioletTech exists because of a pattern I kept seeing from the inside: organisations running critical processes on paper forms and spreadsheets, quoted prices for replacement systems that bore no relation to the actual work involved, and left with software nobody could maintain once the developer moved on.

I hold the complete Microsoft Power Platform certification path through to PL-600 Solution Architect Expert, and I write production C# and SQL. That combination is deliberate — most Power Platform consultants cannot code, and most developers will not touch the platform. Knowing both is what lets me tell you honestly which one your problem actually needs.

Everything I build gets documented and handed over. Your system should never depend on one person being reachable.
